# Intentions and ways to play

ALTER EVEREST is an open world, not a list of prescribed missions. The
following ideas are prompts for human imagination. They do not limit what a
human may ask or give any example special physics.

## The first real question

After the local rehearsal, the agent obtains a human intention before planning
a real candidate. If the human has not already supplied one, ask:

> What would you like this climber to change or attempt in the world, and how
> much survival risk is acceptable?

Offer a varied sample rather than making the human learn the command surface:

> It could be a safe Hello World, one stone on the summit, a Newcomer Village
> contribution, a villa district, a faster summit passage, a bridge, tunnel,
> cellar or helipad, a wall or maze, a deliberate one-way death, or the repair
> or dismantling of somebody else's structure.

If the human already gave a clear intention, do not repeat the generic
question. Reflect the goal and ask only about choices that materially change
the result: approximate site, scale, acceptable survival risk, desired social
coordination, or whether an existing structure should be preserved.

Ask about risk, not a `round-trip` or `one-way` switch. The submitted terminal
stance determines survival.

## Newcomer Village

When the human has no project in mind, offer these fixed starter missions:

| Mission | Intention |
| --- | --- |
| **Safe Hello World** | Import one Base stone, place it legally beyond the Camp boundary, and return alive. |
| **Newcomer Village foundation** | Make one useful stable alteration in the accessible Base Camp outskirts that another expedition could extend. |
| **First trail improvement** | Add one marker, step or support near an observed route without blocking ordinary passage. |
| **First Community Build contribution** | Inspect open Builds with the human and complete one small compatible contribution. |

“Newcomer Village” is an onboarding project family, not reserved land. Current
world occupancy and open Build discussions determine whether the agent should
found a small independent piece, extend an existing cluster, or join a named
Build. Starting a shared Build still requires the human's ambition.

These are real canonical expeditions. They come after the non-canonical
rehearsal in [First local exact expedition](FIRST-EXPEDITION.md).

## Small actions and personal expeditions

- Prove the complete system with the safest useful one-stone alteration.
- Carry a marker to a scenic point and return.
- Put one stone on the summit.
- Quarry one exposed voxel and turn it into a step, seat or sign.
- Recover a dangerous loose stone to Base.
- Repair one support or replace one missing piece.
- Cross the mountain and accept a one-way death.
- Choose a meaningful place to die and leave a tombstone.
- Move, recover or dismantle somebody else's alteration.

Even a tiny intention can be difficult when the exact route, interaction
reach, carrying load, Endurance, support and return all matter.

## Settlements and landmarks

- Grow Newcomer Village outside Base Camp.
- Build a mountainside villa district.
- Make shelters, terraces, courtyards, viewing platforms or memorial gardens.
- Construct a summit beacon or helicopter landing pad.
- Wrap the accessible side of Base Camp in a wall while respecting the
  protected Spawn Core.
- Create gates, arches, towers, stairs or recognizable public art.

Large structures normally span many expeditions. Use a
[Community Build](COMMUNITY.md) when the project needs a shared location,
design conversation or many climbers.

## Routes and infrastructure

- Search for and construct a faster route toward the summit.
- Improve a common track with steps, markers, retaining walls and shelters.
- Bridge a crevasse, trench or artificial gap.
- Excavate a supported tunnel, cellar, refuge or mountain passage.
- Build a gatehouse or a narrow physical toll passage.
- Redirect traffic through a designed cave, arch or switchback.
- Design a maze around a travelled area.

The environment has no abstract road, toll, ownership or access-control
mechanic. These intentions must be expressed through actual 20 cm matter and
legal traversable geometry. Other climbers may route around, modify or remove
the result.

## Counterplay and conflict

- Dismantle a visible building.
- Block or complicate a commonly used track.
- Undermine an opponent's plan without creating an illegal intermediate
  structure.
- Open a new bypass around a wall or gate.
- Repair vandalism or recover important matter.
- Move a landmark and create a competing centre of activity.

Verifier legality is not social approval. Structures have no permanent owner,
and Community Build intentions are not locks. Before changing recognizable
shared work, inspect its context and decide with the human whether the goal is
maintenance, competition, experimentation or deliberate destruction.

## From ambition to executable expedition

The human chooses the desired difference in the world. The agent then:

1. identifies the relevant current site, structure, route or matter;
2. decides whether this is one expedition or a multi-expedition Build;
3. observes only the world area needed to plan;
4. searches for an exact legal route and ordered actions;
5. compares viable solutions by the human's actual priorities;
6. submits the complete candidate only after full verification and freshness
   checks.

An ambition may be much larger than one candidate. Each accepted candidate is
still one atomic expedition, and the next expedition plans against the world
that now exists.
